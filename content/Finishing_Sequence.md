# Finishing Sequence

## Summary
The Finishing Sequence is the concluding section of the Ashtanga Yoga practice. It consists of deep backbends, inversions, and finally, profound rest. Its purpose is to calm the nervous system, balance the energy generated during the practice, and shift the body into a state of profound integration.

## Detail
The Finishing Sequence is uniform across all Ashtanga series (Primary, Intermediate, Advanced), acting as the crucial closing ritual.

The sequence progresses through several distinct energetic shifts:
1. **Backbending**: The sequence begins with [[Urdhva Dhanurasana|Urdhva Dhanurasana]] (Upward Bow/Wheel Pose), opening the front of the body and stimulating the nervous system. This is often followed by a final forward fold ([[Paschimottanasana|Paschimottanasana]]) to neutralize the spine.
2. **Inversions (The Cooling Poses)**: 
   - **[[Salamba Sarvangasana|Salamba Sarvangasana]]** (Shoulderstand): Often called the "Queen of Asanas," it cools the body and stimulates the thyroid gland through the chin lock (Jalandhara Bandha).
   - **[[Halasana|Halasana]]** (Plow Pose) & **[[Karnapidasana|Karnapidasana]]** (Ear Pressure Pose): Deep flexion of the spine and neck.
   - **[[Urdhva Padmasana|Urdhva Padmasana]]** & **[[Pindasana|Pindasana]]**: Inverted lotus variations that compress the internal organs and deepen the sense of internalization.
   - **[[Matsyasana|Matsyasana]]** (Fish Pose) & **[[Uttana Padasana|Uttana Padasana]]**: The counter-poses to Shoulderstand, extending the neck and opening the chest.
   - **[[Sirsasana|Sirsasana]]** (Headstand): Known as the "King of Asanas," it builds focus, reverses blood flow, and demands immense structural integrity from the shoulders and core, taking pressure off the cervical spine.
3. **Seated Closing**: 
   - **[[Baddha Padmasana & Yoga Mudra|Baddha Padmasana & Yoga Mudra]]**: Sealing the energy of the practice.
   - **[[Padmasana & Utpluthih|Padmasana]]** (Lotus Pose): Deep meditation and breath control (Pranayama).
   - **[[Padmasana & Utpluthih|Utpluthih]]**: The final burst of effort, lifting the body off the floor using core strength, burning off any remaining impurities.
4. **Integration**:
   - **[[Savasana|Savasana]]** (Corpse Pose): The ultimate posture of surrender. It is crucial for destroying fatigue, calming the mind, and allowing the nervous system to absorb the benefits of the practice.
