# Seated Sequence

## Summary
The Seated Sequence is the core therapeutic section of the Primary Series (Yoga Chikitsa). It focuses deeply on forward folds, intense hip rotation, and core strengthening, punctuated by jumping back and jumping through (Vinyasa) to maintain internal heat.

## Detail
The Seated Sequence begins once the body has been thoroughly heated by the Sun Salutations and grounded by the Standing Sequence. The sequence relies on anterior pelvic tilt to safely execute intense forward folds.

Key components of the Seated Sequence include:
1. **[[Dandasana|Dandasana]] & [[Paschimottanasana|Paschimottanasana]] (A, B, C, D)**: The foundation of seated postures, establishing alignment and intensely stretching the back body (the "West").
2. **[[Purvottanasana|Purvottanasana]]**: The counter-pose to forward folding, stretching the front body (the "East").
3. **Half-Lotus & Asymmetrical Folds**: Poses like [[Ardha Baddha Padma Paschimottanasana|Ardha Baddha Padma Paschimottanasana]], [[Triang Mukha Eka Pada Paschimottanasana|Triang Mukha Eka Pada Paschimottanasana]], and the [[Janu Sirsasana|Janu Sirsasana]] series (A, B, C) introduce complex knee and hip rotations combined with forward bending.
4. **[[Marichyasana|Marichyasana]] Series (A, B, C, D)**: Deep hip flexion and spinal twisting, often involving binding the arms around the legs.
5. **[[Navasana|Navasana]] (Boat Pose)**: Repeated core activation.
6. **Arm Balances & Deep Hip Flexion**: [[Bhujapidasana|Bhujapidasana]], [[Kurmasana|Kurmasana]], and [[Supta Kurmasana|Supta Kurmasana]] demand extreme flexibility in the hips and hamstrings, combined with arm strength.
7. **Lotus Variations**: [[Garbha Pindasana|Garbha Pindasana]] and [[Kukkutasana|Kukkutasana]] challenge balance, core strength, and the integrity of the Padmasana (Lotus) position.
8. **Wide Angle & Reclined Folds**: [[Baddha Konasana|Baddha Konasana]], [[Upavistha Konasana|Upavistha Konasana]], and [[Supta Konasana|Supta Konasana]] stretch the adductors and hamstrings from different angles.
9. **Final Seated Core & Backbends**: [[Supta Padangusthasana|Supta Padangusthasana]], [[Ubhaya Padangusthasana|Ubhaya Padangusthasana]], [[Urdhva Mukha Paschimottanasana|Urdhva Mukha Paschimottanasana]], and [[Setu Bandhasana|Setu Bandhasana]] prepare the body for the intense backbends of the Finishing Sequence.

The continuous Vinyasa between sides and poses is crucial to clear lactic acid, reset the spine, and maintain the continuous flow of Prana.
