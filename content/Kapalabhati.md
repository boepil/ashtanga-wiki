# Kapalabhati (Skull Shining Breath)

*Kapālabhāti* (*Kapāla* = skull, *bhāti* = lustre or shining) is a powerful respiratory cleansing technique designed to purify the skull, brain, and respiratory passages. It stokes the internal fire (*agni*) and is a vital preliminary practice for advanced pranayama.

## Method

Kapālabhāti reverses the usual mechanics of breathing by using a rhythmic "bellows" action.

1. **Postural Foundation:** Sit in a stable meditative posture (*Padmasana* or *Siddhasana*) with an erect spine.
2. **Active Exhalation:** The exhalation is vigorous and forceful. It is achieved by quickly snapping the abdominal muscles inward, blasting air out through the nostrils.
3. **Passive Inhalation:** The inhalation is slow, silent, and completely passive. As the abdominal muscles relax, the lungs naturally refill with air.
4. **Rhythm:** One cycle is one active exhale followed by one passive inhale. Beginners start with 12–25 rounds and increase gradually.

## Benefits

- **Respiratory Hygiene:** Dries up excess phlegm (*kapha*) and forcefully clears the respiratory tract and sinuses.
- **Brain Cleansing:** Clears the "winding passages" of the brain, leading to the mental clarity and "lustre" from which the practice gets its name.
- **Metabolic Fire:** Rapidly stokes the digestive fire (*Jathara Agni*), making it an excellent practice for those with a lethargic constitution.
- **Energetic:** Removes obstructions at the entrance to the central channel, preparing the body for the awakening of Kundalini.

## Safety and Contraindications

> [!CAUTION]
> Kapālabhāti is forceful and rapidly alters internal pressure. Stop immediately if you experience nosebleeds, throbbing ears, or dizziness.

- **Strict Contraindications:**
  - **Pregnancy:** The rapid abdominal pumping is unsafe.
  - **Cardiovascular Issues:** Unsafe for those with high blood pressure or heart conditions.
  - **Cranial Pressure:** Avoid if suffering from glaucoma, detached retinas, or inner ear infections.
