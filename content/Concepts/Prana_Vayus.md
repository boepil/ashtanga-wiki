# The Five Prana Vayus

In the subtle anatomy of yoga, *Prana* (the universal life force) is divided into five primary energetic currents or "winds" known as the **Prana Vayus**. These currents govern specific physical functions and psychological energies within the body.

## The Five Winds

1. **Prana Vayu (Inward/Receiving)**: Located primarily in the chest and heart region. It governs the intake of breath, food, and sensory information. It is the energy of reception and vitality.
2. **Apana Vayu (Downward/Eliminating)**: Located in the pelvis and lower abdomen. It governs all downward and outward movements, including elimination (feces, urine, menstruation), childbirth, and the exhalation of breath. It is the grounding energy.
3. **Samana Vayu (Equalizing/Digesting)**: Located in the navel region. It governs the digestion and assimilation of food, as well as the processing of thoughts and emotions. It balances Prana and Apana.
4. **Udana Vayu (Upward/Expressing)**: Located in the throat and head. It governs upward movement, speech, physical growth, and the elevation of consciousness.
5. **Vyana Vayu (Pervading/Circulating)**: Moves outward from the center to the periphery, pervading the entire body. It governs circulation, coordination, and the nervous system.

## Integration in Practice

Understanding the Vayus is crucial for deep [[Pranayama]] and the engagement of energetic locks (Bandhas). For instance, Mula Bandha lifts the *Apana Vayu* upward, while Jalandhara Bandha directs the *Prana Vayu* downward, forcing them to meet in the navel (*Samana*) and eventually enter the central channel ([[Sushumna]]) to awaken higher consciousness.
