# Nauli (Abdominal Churning)

**Nauli** is a [shatkarma](/Shatkarma_Overview) involving the isolation and rotation of the *rectus abdominis* muscles to massage the internal abdominal organs. The *Hatha Yoga Pradipika* claims it removes all diseases. It is a powerful form of internal visceral manipulation that restores balance to the digestive and energetic systems.

## Role in the Shatkarmas

Nauli is the fifth of the six Shatkarmas. Its primary purpose is to bring the three doshas (Kapha, Pitta, Vata) into equilibrium. Traditional texts emphasize it as crucial for those with excess fat or phlegm (Kapha), though unnecessary for those already balanced.

## The Four Stages

1. **Uddiyana Bandha** (foundation) — Exhale fully, draw the abdomen in and up under the ribcage.
2. **Madhyama Nauli** (central isolation) — Contract only the central band of the rectus abdominis into a vertical pillar.
3. **Vama Nauli** (left isolation) — Contract only the left abdominal muscles.
4. **Dakshina Nauli** (right isolation) — Contract only the right abdominal muscles.

Once isolation is mastered, the muscles are rotated in a fluid, circular churning motion — like the spin cycle of a washing machine.

## How to Practice

Stand with feet apart, knees slightly bent, hands on the thighs. Exhale fully and engage Uddiyana Bandha. Press the hands into the thighs to isolate the rectus abdominis. Once stable, shift pressure to one side to isolate that side. For the churning motion, smoothly transfer the contraction from left to center to right in a circular wave. The transversus abdominis provides the pressure and control needed for the movement.

## Benefits

### Digestive Health
- **Stokes Jathara Agni** (digestive fire) — "bakes" away impurities and diseases of the stomach and spleen
- **Deep intestinal massage** — tones all abdominal organs
- **Promotes peristalsis** — highly effective for alleviating constipation
- **Stimulates digestion** and removes internal imbalances

### Visceral Manipulation & Tissue Health
- **Breaks up adhesions** in connective tissues and fascial compartments surrounding abdominal organs
- **Restores organ motility** — ensures organs slide and glide fluidly
- **Tones the deep core** — strengthens the transversus abdominis

### Circulation & Detoxification
- **Irrigates blood and lymph** throughout the abdominal cavities
- **Flushes toxins** from cells, tissues, and intercellular fluid
- **Resets metabolic rhythms** across cardiovascular, digestive, and endocrine systems

### Energetic & Subtle Body
- **Increases Tejas** (inner fire / radiance)
- **Stimulates Manipura Chakra** (solar plexus)
- **Balances the koshas** — targets *annamaya* and *pranamaya* layers
- **Clears Kapha stagnation** — both physical and energetic
- **Manages Samana Vayu** — the equalizing energy of digestion and assimilation

## Contraindications

> [!CAUTION]
> Nauli is an advanced practice. Do **not** attempt without first mastering Uddiyana Bandha. Strictly prohibited during **pregnancy, menstruation**, or with **hiatal hernia, active abdominal inflammation, high blood pressure, heart disease, or recent abdominal surgery**. Must be performed on a completely **empty stomach** (wait at least 6 hours after a meal). Stop immediately if you experience dizziness, fainting, or sharp internal pain. Should only be learned under the direct supervision of an experienced teacher.
