# Visual Asana Dictionary - Advanced Series

The Advanced Series (*Sthira Bhaga*) is divided into four parts: A, B, C, and D. These series integrate the strength and flexibility developed in the Primary and Intermediate series, focusing on advanced arm balances, deep backbends, and complex transitions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Advanced Series should only be practiced under the guidance of a qualified Ashtanga teacher after mastery of the Primary and Intermediate series.

## 01. Advanced A (Third Series)
Focuses on advanced arm balances and deep hip/spinal flexibility.
