# Genealogy of Modern Yoga: Lineages and Traditions

While the Ashtanga Vinyasa tradition is highly influential, modern yoga is a diverse "family tree" with multiple roots. Most global postural yoga stems from two primary Indian nodes: the **Krishnamacharya Lineage** (Mysore) and the **Sivananda Lineage** (Rishikesh).

## 1. The Krishnamacharya Lineage (The Mysore Branch)
Commonly called the "Father of Modern Yoga," T. Krishnamacharya synthesized Hatha Yoga with physical culture. His students became the primary architects of modern postural practice.

| Tradition | Founder | Key Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ashtanga Vinyasa** | [[K_Pattabhi_Jois]] | High-intensity, fixed sequences, breath-movement synchronization (Vinyasa). |
| **Iyengar Yoga** | [[BKS_Iyengar]] | Extreme precision, alignment, long holds, and the use of props (blocks, straps). |
| **Viniyoga** | T.K.V. Desikachar | Therapeutic, one-on-one application, breath-centered, highly individualized. |
| **Vinyasa Krama** | Srivatsa Ramaswami | Systematic sequences with hundreds of variations, following a slower pace than Ashtanga. |
| **Indra Devi Yoga** | Indra Devi | Focused on relaxation, stress relief, and "Yoga for Americans." |

## 2. The Sivananda Lineage (The Rishikesh Branch)
Founded by Swami Sivananda Saraswati, a former medical doctor. This lineage emphasizes a "Yoga of Synthesis," integrating all four classical paths (Action, Knowledge, Devotion, Meditation).

| Tradition | Key Figure | Key Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sivananda Yoga** | Swami Vishnudevananda | Standardized "12 Basic Asanas," 5 Points of Yoga (Proper Exercise, Breathing, Relaxation, Diet, Positive Thinking/Meditation). |
| **Integral Yoga** | Swami Satchidananda | Gentle, spiritual focus, multi-path synthesis. Famous for opening the 1969 Woodstock festival. |
| **Bihar School** | Swami Satyananda | Integration of Tantra, Hatha, and Raja Yoga. Known for developing the modern **Yoga Nidra** technique. |
| **Satyananda Yoga** | Swami Niranjanananda | Continuing the Bihar tradition with a focus on holistic lifestyle and "Yoga from head to toe." |

## 3. The Scientific & Research Branch
These schools focused on validating yoga through the lens of modern medicine and physiology.

*   **Kaivalyadhama (Lonavla):** Founded by **Swami Kuvalayananda**. The first institution to conduct scientific research on asana and pranayama. They "medicalized" yoga, making it palatable to the scientific community.
*   **The Yoga Institute (Mumbai):** Founded by **Shri Yogendra**. Focused on bringing yoga to householders and secularizing the practice for health.

## 4. Other Influential Lineages
*   **Himalayan Institute:** Founded by **Swami Rama**. Known for integrating yoga with Western psychology and the "Science of Breath."
*   **Self-Realization Fellowship (SRF):** Founded by **Paramahansa Yogananda**. Primarily teaches **Kriya Yoga** (meditative techniques) rather than postural Hatha Yoga.
*   **Kripalu Yoga:** Founded by **Amrit Desai**. Focuses on "meditation in motion" and following the body's internal wisdom rather than strict external alignment.
*   **Kundalini Yoga (3HO):** Founded by **Yogi Bhajan**. Synthesizes Hatha techniques with Sikh mantras and rapid breathing (Breath of Fire) to "awaken the Kundalini."

## 5. Modern Synthesis & Hybrid Styles
Most contemporary Western styles are "Vinyasa Flow" hybrids that mix elements of Ashtanga (movement) and Iyengar (alignment).
*   **Jivamukti Yoga:** (Sharon Gannon & David Life) Ashtanga-based with strong emphasis on veganism, activism, and Sanskrit chanting.
*   **Rocket Yoga:** (Larry Schultz) A faster, "remixed" version of the Ashtanga Primary and Intermediate series.
*   **Yin Yoga:** (Paul Grilley & Sarah Powers) Focuses on deep connective tissues (fascia) through long, passive holds, influenced by Chinese Medicine.

## Summary: Focus Differences
*   **Krishnamacharya Schools:** Often more focused on **Asana (posture)**, structural alignment, and the physical-energetic body.
*   **Sivananda Schools:** Often more focused on **Spirituality**, lifestyle, relaxation, and a balanced synthesis of all yoga paths.
