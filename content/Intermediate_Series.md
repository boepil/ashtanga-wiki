# Intermediate Series (Nadi Shodhana)

## Summary
The Intermediate Series, known as *Nadi Shodhana* (Nerve Cleansing), is the second sequence in Ashtanga Yoga. It focuses on purifying and strengthening the nervous system and the energy channels (nadis) through intense backbends, deep twists, and arm balances.

## Detail
While the Primary Series (Yoga Chikitsa) detoxifies the physical body and addresses structural imbalances, the Intermediate Series works deeper into the subtle body and nervous system. It requires the foundation of strength, flexibility, and bandha control established in the Primary Series.

The sequence structure is:
1. **Opening**: Surya Namaskar A & B and the Standing Sequence (up to Parsvottanasana).
2. **Core Sequence (Backbends & Twists)**: 
   - Deep spinal twists like **Pasasana** (Noose Pose).
   - Intense backbends that open the front of the body and strengthen the spine, including **Salabhasana** (Locust), **Bhekasana** (Frog), **Dhanurasana** (Bow), **Ustrasana** (Camel), and the pinnacle backbend, **Kapotasana** (Pigeon Pose).
3. **Arm Balances & Deep Core**:
   - Poses like **Bakasana** (Crane), **Tittibhasana** (Firefly), and **Pincha Mayurasana** (Forearm Balance) demand intense core strength and shoulder stability.
4. **Legs Behind Head (Eka Pada Sirsasana series)**: Deep hip opening and forward folding to counter the intense backbending.
5. **Final Twists & Balances**: Poses like **Mayurasana**, **Vatayanasana**, and **Gomukhasana** integrate the series.
6. **Finishing**: The sequence concludes with the standard Finishing Sequence (Urdhva Dhanurasana, inversions, and Padmasana).

Practicing Nadi Shodhana is deeply heating and can release stored emotions or psychological tension due to its profound effect on the spine and nervous system.
