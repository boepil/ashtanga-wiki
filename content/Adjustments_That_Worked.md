## Distinguishing Types of Breakthroughs

When logging adjustments, try to categorize the type of breakthrough you experienced:

### 1. The Mechanical Shift
Primarily concerns the biomechanical alignment and structural integrity of the pose.
- **Indicators:** Even weight distribution, cessation of joint "jamming," or feeling skeletal support without muscular strain.

### 2. The Neurological Breakthrough
Involves a shift in proprioception—the internal sense of how the body moves in space. It is the retraining of the nervous system to let go of old habits.
- **Indicators:** A specific muscle or area that felt "dead" or inaccessible suddenly feels "awake" and responsive. The teacher's touch gives "germination" to new cellular awareness.

### 3. The Energetic Breakthrough
A transition from the gross physical body to the subtle energetic body (*sūkṣma-śarīra*).
- **Indicators:** A sudden surge of heat (*tapas*), a feeling of "blissful fusion," or a moment of being "thoughtfully thoughtless." You clearly perceive the movement of *Prana* through the *Nadis*, piercing through physical resistance.
