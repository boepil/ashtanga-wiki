# Inspiration and Insights

Integrating practice and theory requires a space for reflecting on insights that arise when the physical practice meets the *Shastras* (sacred texts). True knowledge is confirmed through direct perception (*Pratyaksha*) on the mat. External inspiration acts as a catalyst for this direct knowledge.

## External Inspiration as a Bridge

The ultimate goal of yoga is to develop a relationship with the **Inner Teacher**—the intuitive controller that perceives truth directly.
- **The Outer Teacher/Texts:** Serve as the "outer shell" or the "rails of argument" for the mind to follow.
- **The Transition:** Eventually, the practitioner stands in their own nature, discovering the wisdom already latent within.
