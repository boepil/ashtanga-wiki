# Jala Neti (Nasal Irrigation)

*Jala Neti* is the Shatkarma focused on purifying the nasal passages and the head. While modern practitioners typically use a Neti pot, classical texts outline multiple variations of water-based purification.

## Traditional Methods

1. **Vyutkrama**: Drawing water in through the nostrils and expelling it through the mouth.
2. **Vamakrama**: Drawing water through one nostril, lifting the face, and expelling it through the opposite nostril (repeated bilaterally).
3. **Sītkrama**: Ingesting water through the mouth and expelling it through the nostrils.

> **Note on Drying:** The practice of Neti must be followed by vigorous breathing, usually **[[Kapalabhati]]**, to dry the nasal passages completely and remove all excess moisture.

## Benefits

### Physical
- **Brain & Skull Cleansing**: Clears out excess phlegm (*kapha*) from the sinuses and is considered a "cleaner of the brain."
- **Respiratory Health**: Alleviates chronic sneezing, runny noses, and clears out allergens and dust.
- **Sensory Enhancement**: Sharpens the sense of smell and reduces cranial pressure, potentially improving eyesight.

### Energetic
- **Cakra Purification**: Specifically used to purify the *Ajna Chakra* (the third eye).
- **Divine Sight**: Traditionally said to bestow a heightened sense of perception or *Divya Dṛṣṭi* ("divine sight").

## Safety and Contraindications

- **Initial Discomfort**: Practitioners often experience a burning sensation or temporary congestion during the first weeks of practice.
- **Not a Daily Requirement**: Nasal kriyas are primarily curative. They should not be practiced blindly every day if the practitioner is already healthy, as they can be habit-forming or irritate the mucous membranes.
