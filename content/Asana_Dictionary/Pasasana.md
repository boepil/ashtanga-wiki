# Pasasana

"Pasa means a noose or cord. The arms are used as a noose to hold the trunk to the legs, hence the name." 

![Pasasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Baddha-Malasana-e1583307499371-210x300.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 267) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Pasasana is the first posture in the Intermediate Series taught by Pattabhi Jois." 
*(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Squat on the floor with the soles and heels completely on the floor. Keep the feet and knees together. Turn the trunk about 90 degrees to one side, twist one arm round both the thighs and turning the other arm from the shoulder clasp hands behind the back." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 267) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Nose (nasagre). Iyengar instructs to "gaze back." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 267) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Movement
Squatting twist, heels on the ground, arms bound around the knees.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Obliques:** "The internal and external obliques are primary movers for the deep spinal rotation." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Quadriceps:** "Maintain the deep squat position and stabilize the knees." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Shoulder Rotators:** "Subscapularis and infraspinatus facilitate the internal rotation required for the bind." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Spinal rotation. Hip flexion (deep squat). Knee flexion. Shoulder internal rotation and extension." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Uddiyana Bandha is essential to create the space in the abdomen for the deep twist and to protect the lumbar spine." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 794) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Torque:** "If the heels are lifted, the force of the twist can translate into the knee joint, risking the meniscus." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 170) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Spinal Compression:** "Twisting without enough vertical length can compress the intervertebral discs." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 172) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Keep the heels firmly on the floor. If they lift, place a folded blanket or mat under them." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Elongate the spine on the inhalation before deepening the twist on the exhalation." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"Pasasana stimulates the abdominal organs and helps to correct the alignment of the spine after the intense forward folds of the Primary Series." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 268) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
