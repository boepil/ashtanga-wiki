# Koundinyasana A (Eka Pada Koundinyasana)

"Koundinya was a sage. This advanced arm balance involves leg extension and spinal twisting."

![Koundinyasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 12) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
An arm balance where the body is twisted, and the legs are extended in opposite directions while balanced on the upper arms.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Spinal Rotation**: Requires a deep twist in the thoracic and lumbar spine to position the legs across the arms.
- **Oblique Strength**: Essential for maintaining the twist and balancing the body's weight.
- **Adductor & Hamstring Lengthening**: The extended legs require significant flexibility and active engagement to stay straight and lifted.
