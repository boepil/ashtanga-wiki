# Galavasana

"Gālava was a sage, a disciple of Visvamitra. This pose is an advanced arm balance with the legs crossed over the arms."

![Galavasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 11) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
An arm balance where one leg is in a "Figure 4" position across the upper arms, and the other leg is extended back (or tucked).

## Anatomical Focus
- **Shoulder Stability**: Serratus anterior and rotator cuff must stabilize the scapulae while bearing weight.
- **Hip Flexibility**: Requires deep external rotation in the "Figure 4" leg to safely place the shin across the triceps.
- **Triceps Strength**: Essential for maintaining the "shelf" for the leg.
