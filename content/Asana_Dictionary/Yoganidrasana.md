# Yoganidrasana

"Yoga means union. Nidra means sleep. Yoganidrasana is the 'Yogic Sleep' pose, as the body is curled into itself like a person sleeping." 

![Yoganidrasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Yoganidrasana.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 297) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Yoganidrasana has seven vinyasas. The 3rd is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1431) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Lie on the back... bend both legs and place them behind the head, crossing the feet. Interlace the fingers behind the lower back. This posture is essentially Dwi Pada Sirsasana performed while lying down." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 297–298) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"trīṇi 3: exhale, place the legs behind the head and clasp the hands over the lower back. Breathe five times." 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 60) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Third eye (nasagre/midbrow)
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Lying on back, both legs behind the neck, feet crossed, hands clasped behind the back.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Hip Rotators:** "Deep engagement of all external rotators to maintain the leg position." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Abdominals:** "Engage to maintain the deep flexion of the hips and to prevent the spine from over-compressing." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Spinal Flexors:** "Active to curl the body into the 'egg' shape." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Extreme hip external rotation and flexion. Knee flexion. Deep spinal flexion." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Mula Bandha and Uddiyana Bandha are vital to contain the Prana within the torso in this highly compressed shape. The suction of Uddiyana facilitates the deep flexion needed." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 807) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Lumbar Herniation:** "Forcing the body into this deep flexion without sufficient hip mobility can put excessive pressure on the lumbar discs." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 285) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Knee Instability:** "Similar to Dwi Pada, the risk of torquing the knee is high if the hips are not open." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 288) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Only attempt the bind if the legs are comfortably behind the neck and the spine is not in pain." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Focus on the relaxation of the nervous system (Nidra) rather than the physical 'closeness' of the pose." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"This asana is said to induce a state of deep internal focus and is highly beneficial for the nervous system, provided the physical prerequisites are met." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 299) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
