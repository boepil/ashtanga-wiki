# Bhekasana

"Bheka means a frog. The action in this asana resembles that of a frog, hence the name." 

![Bhekasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 203) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Bhekasana is listed as the fourth posture in the Intermediate Series." 
*(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Lie full length on the floor on the stomach... Exhale, bend the knees and move the heels towards the hips. Hold the sole of the right foot with the right hand and the sole of the left foot with the left hand. Turn the hands so that the palms touch the upper part of the feet and the toes and fingers point to the head." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 203) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Exhale, lift the head and trunk from the floor and look up. Push the hands further down and bring the toes and heels closer to the ground." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 203) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Nose (nasagre)
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Lying on stomach, knees bent, feet pressed down by hips with hands. Chest lifted.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Quadriceps:** "Intense stretch in all four heads, especially the rectus femoris." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Psoas Major:** "Stretched as the hips stay grounded while the torso lifts." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Erector Spinae:** "Active to maintain the lift of the chest." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Knee flexion (intense). Hip extension and external rotation. Spinal extension." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Mula Bandha is vital to protect the knees; by engaging the pelvic floor, you create a line of energy that helps manage the intense pull on the quadriceps." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 797) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Compression:** "Pressing the heels down without proper hip rotation can compress the knee joint, risking the meniscus." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 190) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Wrist Strain:** "The required 'frog hand' position (fingers pointing forward) can strain the wrists if they are not sufficiently mobile." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 192) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "If the knees feel sharp pain, do one leg at a time (Ardha Bhekasana)." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Keep the neck long and gaze at the tip of the nose to avoid neck strain." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"Bhekasana is excellent for developing the arches of the feet and relieving gout or other pains in the knees." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 204) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
