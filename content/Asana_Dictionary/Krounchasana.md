# Krounchasana

"Krouncha means a heron. It is also the name of a mountain... The raised leg resembles the outstretched neck and head of a heron." 

![Krounchasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Krounchasana.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 242) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Krounchasana is listed as the second asana in the Intermediate Series." 
*(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Sit on the floor... Bend the right leg at the knee and move the right foot back. Place the right foot by the side of the hip joint in Virasana. Join the knees together. Exhale, bend the left knee, hold the left foot with both hands and raise the left leg up vertically." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 242–243) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Rest the chin on the knee of the left leg. Do not lift the bent knee off the floor while the chin is touching the knee of the lifted leg." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 243) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Toes (padayoragra). Yoga Makaranda defaults to midbrow if unspecified. 
*(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Movement
One leg in Virasana (bent back by hip), other leg straight and pulled towards the face. Chin to knee.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Hamstrings:** "Semitendinosus and semimembranosus are intensely stretched in the vertical leg." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Quadriceps (Virasana leg):** "The rectus femoris is in a state of intense eccentric stretch." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Hip Flexors:** "Iliopsoas engages to pull the torso and leg together." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Hip flexion (lifted leg). Hip internal rotation and flexion (Virasana leg). Knee flexion (Virasana leg). Knee extension (lifted leg)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Mula Bandha and Uddiyana Bandha are crucial to stabilize the pelvis, preventing it from tilting unevenly due to the asymmetrical leg position." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 795) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Torque:** "In the Virasana leg, if the foot is not properly aligned with the shin, the knee joint can experience shearing forces, risking the medial collateral ligament." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 175) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Hamstring Tear:** "Aggressively pulling the straight leg towards the face without sufficient warmth can cause micro-tears at the ischial tuberosity (sit-bone)." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 178) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Ensure the knees are touching. If the Virasana hip is lifting, sit on a block or blanket." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Focus on keeping the spine long rather than rounding to touch the chin to the knee." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"This asana effectively combines the opening of Virasana with the deep hamstring stretch of Paschimottanasana, targeting the entire lower body." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 244) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
