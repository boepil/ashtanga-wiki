# Bhairavasana

"Bhairava is the name of a formidable manifestation of Shiva. This pose is a side-lying posture with one leg behind the head."

![Bhairavasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 9) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A side-reclining pose where one leg is behind the head and the body is balanced on the side.

## Anatomical Focus
- **External Hip Rotation**: High demand on the hip capsule and gluteal flexibility.
- **Spinal Rotation**: The torso must rotate to accommodate the leg-behind-head position while maintaining a side-lying balance.
- **Neck Safety**: Avoid pushing the head forward with the leg; the leg should be supported by the upper back/trapezius.
