# Ardha Matsyendrasana (Half Lord of the Fishes)

![Ardha-Matsyendrasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Ardha-Matsyendrasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Ardha means half. Matsyendra was a legendary yogi and founder of Hatha Yoga. This asana is the simplified version of the full Matsyendrasana." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 259) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Ardha Matsyendrasana has thirteen vinyasas. The 7th is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1429) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Sit on the floor... bend the left leg and place the left heel against the right side of the perineum. Bend the right leg and place the right foot on the outer side of the left thigh, so the right ankle touches the left thigh. Turn the trunk 90 degrees to the right. Pass the left arm around the right knee and hold the right big toe." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 259–260) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Side (parshva drishti) - looking over the shoulder.
*(raw PDF)*

## Movement
Seated twist, one leg folded under, other leg over, deep bind with arms.
*(raw PDF)*

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Obliques:** "The primary drivers of the spinal rotation." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Erector Spinae:** "Maintain the verticality of the spine to ensure the rotation is distributed evenly." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Piriformis:** "Stretched in the top leg as the hip is moved into deep flexion and rotation." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Spinal rotation. Hip flexion and external rotation (top leg). Hip flexion and internal rotation (bottom leg). Shoulder internal rotation and retraction (in the bind)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Uddiyana Bandha is crucial to prevent the 'snowballing' of the lumbar spine. By pulling the navel in and up, you create the necessary space for the thoracic spine to rotate safely." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 805) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Disc Compression:** "Twisting while slumped (flexed spine) puts dangerous uneven pressure on the intervertebral discs." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 265) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **SI Joint Irritation:** "If the hips are not grounded, the twist can migrate into the sacroiliac joint, causing sharp pain." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 268) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Ensure both sit-bones are firmly on the floor before initiating the twist." *(raw PDF)*
- "Rule: Always find length on the inhale and depth on the exhale." *(raw PDF)*

## Teaching Notes
"This asana is famous for its detoxifying effects on the liver and kidneys. It is the most complete twist in the Intermediate Series." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 262) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
