# Mayurasana

"Mayura means a peacock. This asana resembles a peacock with its tail spread." 

![Mayurasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Mayurasana.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 283) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Mayurasana has nine vinyasas. The 5th is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1435) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Kneel on the floor... join the palms and place them on the floor with the fingers pointing towards the feet. Lean forward and place the elbows on the abdomen. Exhale, stretch the legs straight and lift them off the floor, balancing the whole body on the hands." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 283–284) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Nose (nasagre)
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Arm balance, elbows in abdomen, body horizontal, legs together and straight.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Erector Spinae:** "Intensely active to keep the legs and torso horizontal against gravity." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Forearm Flexors:** "Active to maintain the wrist position under significant load." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Abdominals:** "Must be firm to support the pressure of the elbows into the viscera." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Wrist extension (extreme). Elbow flexion. Shoulder protraction. Hip extension. Knee extension." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Uddiyana Bandha is paradoxically difficult in Mayurasana because the elbows are pressing directly into the abdominal wall. A 'micro-suction' is needed to create a shelf for the elbows while protecting the internal organs." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 811) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Wrist Impingement:** "The weight of the whole body on the wrists in extreme extension can lead to ligament strain." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 325) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Visceral Compression:** "Improper placement of the elbows can cause discomfort or bruising of the internal organs." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 328) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Keep the elbows close together in the center of the abdomen." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Lean forward enough so that the head balances the weight of the legs." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"Mayurasana is famous for its digestive benefits, as the pressure on the abdomen stimulates the blood flow to the digestive organs. It is said to neutralize poisons in the body." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 286) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
