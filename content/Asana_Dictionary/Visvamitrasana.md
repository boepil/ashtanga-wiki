# Visvamitrasana

"Viśvāmitra was a king who became a celebrated sage. This pose is a complex combination of an arm balance, a twist, and a deep hip opener."

![Visvamitrasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 5) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
Visvamitrasana is a side plank variation where the bottom leg is extended and the top arm holds the foot, while the other leg is positioned over the upper arm of the weight-bearing side.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Hip Flexors & Hamstrings**: Maximum demand on lengthening while the weight-bearing arm stabilizes the shoulder girdle.
- **Core Stability**: Requires intense oblique engagement to maintain the lateral orientation of the torso.
- **Scapular Stabilization**: The serratus anterior must fire strongly to keep the shoulder blade from "winging" under the weight of the leg.

## Vinyasa Entry
Usually entered from a lunge or a side plank variation.
