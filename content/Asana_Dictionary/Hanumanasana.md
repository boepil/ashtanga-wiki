# Hanumanasana

"Hanuman is the monkey god, son of Pavana (the wind). This pose is the front split, commemorating Hanuman's giant leap across the ocean."

![Hanumanasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 12) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A full front split where the legs are extended in opposite directions, requiring maximum hip flexibility.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Hamstring & Hip Flexor Lengthening**: The front leg hamstrings and the back leg psoas/quadriceps are at their maximum physiological length.
- **Hip Stabilization**: "Maximum demand on hip stabilizers to keep the pelvis squared." [19]
- **Core Engagement**: Essential to maintain an upright spine and prevent excessive arching in the lumbar region.

## Connections
- [[Prasarita Padottanasana]] — Related symmetrical leg lengthening.
- [[Utthita Hasta Padangusthasana]] — Related hamstring flexibility.
