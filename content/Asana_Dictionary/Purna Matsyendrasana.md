# Purna Matsyendrasana

"Matsyendra was a legendary sage and the founder of Hatha Yoga. Purna means full. This is the full version of the seated spinal twist."

![Purna Matsyendrasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 12) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A deep seated twist where one foot is in Padmasana (Lotus) and the other leg is stepped over it, with a full bind around the knee.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Internal Organ Massage**: "Creates a 'wringing' effect on the abdominal organs to stimulate digestion and detoxification." [13, 14]
- **Spinal Rotation**: Maximum rotation of the vertebral column. Requires axial extension before twisting to protect the disks.
- **Hip Flexibility**: Combines deep external rotation (Lotus leg) with adduction and internal rotation (top leg).
