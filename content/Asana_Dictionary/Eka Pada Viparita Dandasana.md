# Eka Pada Viparita Dandasana

"Eka means one, Pada means foot. This is the One-Legged Inverted Staff Pose."

![Eka Pada Viparita Dandasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 15) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A variation of *Dwi Pada Viparita Dandasana* where one leg is lifted vertically while the body remains in the deep backbend.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Asymmetrical Backbending**: Requires stability in the grounded leg and hip while the other leg reaches toward the ceiling.
- **Hip Flexor Lengthening**: The grounded leg's hip flexors (psoas) are under intense stretch.
- **Shoulder Girdle Integrity**: The forearms must stay firmly pressed into the floor to support the spine.
