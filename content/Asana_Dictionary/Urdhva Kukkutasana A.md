# Urdhva Kukkutasana A

"Urdhva means upward and Kukkuta means a rooster. This is an advanced arm balance with the legs in Lotus (Padmasana) lifted high."

![Kukkutasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Kukkutasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 9) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
An arm balance where the legs are in Padmasana and the whole body is lifted off the floor, with the knees pulled high toward the armpits.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Triceps & Core Strength**: "Demands significant triceps and deep core strength to maintain the lift." [10]
- **Abdominal Compression**: Requires intense engagement of the rectus abdominis and psoas to pull the Lotus legs toward the torso.
- **Wrist Stability**: High demand on the wrists due to the vertical load and the shift in center of gravity.
