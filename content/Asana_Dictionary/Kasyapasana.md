# Kasyapasana

"Kaśyapa is the name of a sage, the son of Marichi. This pose involves a reclining balance with one leg behind the head and a bind."

![Kasyapasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 5) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A side-reclining balance that combines the "leg-behind-head" (*Eka Pada Sirsasana*) position with a side plank bind.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Deep Hip Rotation**: Requires significant external rotation of the femur to safely place the leg behind the head.
- **Shoulder Stability**: The weight-bearing arm must maintain integrity while the body twists to find the bind.
- **Spinal Flexibility**: Involves a lateral stretch combined with a rotation.
