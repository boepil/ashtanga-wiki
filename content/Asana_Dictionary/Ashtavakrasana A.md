# Ashtavakrasana A

"Aṣṭāvakra was a sage who was born with eight crooks in his body. This arm balance involves a deep twist and hooked legs."

![Ashtavakrasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 12) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
An arm balance performed by hooking the legs together and extending them to one side while the body is twisted and balanced on the arms.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Deep Oblique Engagement**: Necessary to maintain the twist and lift the hips off the floor.
- **Shoulder & Wrist Stability**: High demand on the upper body due to the asymmetrical and twisted orientation.
- **Adductor Integration**: The legs must "hug" the upper arm to maintain the bind.
