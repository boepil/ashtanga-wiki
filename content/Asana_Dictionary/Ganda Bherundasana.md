# Ganda Bherundasana

"Ganda means a cheek or a side of the face. Bherunda means terrible or formidable. This is a formidable chin-stand backbend."

![Ganda Bherundasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 12) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A chin-stand balance where the legs are in Padmasana (Lotus) or extended, and the back is deeply arched.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Cervical Spine Safety**: The weight must be distributed between the hands and the chin, avoiding excessive pressure on the neck.
- **Shoulder Girdle Stability**: The triceps and serratus anterior must stabilize the arms to support the torso.
- **Thoracic Mobility**: Requires significant opening of the chest and upper back.
