# Viparita Shalabhasana

"Viparita means inverted, and Shalabha means a locust. This is the Inverted Locust Pose."

![Viparita Shalabhasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 12) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
An advanced backbend performed from a chin-stand or chest-stand position, where the feet reach over the head toward the floor.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Maximum Spinal Extension**: Requires extreme flexibility in the entire vertebral column, especially the thoracic spine.
- **Chest & Throat Opening**: The weight is borne on the chin, chest, and shoulders, requiring length in the anterior neck muscles and pectorals.
- **Deep Core Engagement**: Essential to control the descent of the feet and protect the spine.
