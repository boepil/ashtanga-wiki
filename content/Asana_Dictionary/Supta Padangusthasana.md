# Supta Padangusthasana (Reclining Big Toe Pose)

![Supta-Padangusthasana-A](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Supta-Padangusthasana-A.png)
![Supta-Padangusthasana-b](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Supta-Padangusthasana-b.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Supta means lying down. Pada is the foot. Angustha means the big toe." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 317) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"This is a two-part asana: the first part has 20 vinyasas and the second has 28 vinyasas." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1432) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"From the 7th vinyasa (lying down), slowly raise the right leg straight up in the 8th vinyasa. Clasp the big toe and do recaka kumbhaka." 
*(Yoga Makaranda, T. Krishnamacharya, p. 1330) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

**Variation A:** "In the 9th vinyasa, pull the leg toward the chest and place the face against the knee." 
*(Yoga Makaranda, T. Krishnamacharya, p. 1330) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

**Variation B:** "In the second part (11th vinyasa), lower the right leg to the floor on the right side while holding the toe." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1433) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
- **A:** Foot
- **B:** To the left side (opposite of the leg)
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
From lying down position bring the right leg up, hold the big toe, the left hand over the left thigh.
**Variation A:** Lift up, chin towards shin bone.
**Variation B:** Take the right leg to the side, turn the head to the left side.
Repeat on the left side.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Hip Flexors:** "Psoas and iliacus engage to lift the leg." *(Science of Yoga, Ann Swanson, p. 309) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Quadriceps:** "Rectus femoris and vastus muscles extend the knee." *(Science of Yoga, Ann Swanson, p. 309) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Hip Extensors (Stretch):** "Hamstrings and gluteus maximus are intensely lengthened." *(Science of Yoga, Ann Swanson, p. 309) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Core Stabilizers:** "Rectus abdominis engages to lift the head and chest towards the knee (Variation A)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Isolated hamstring lengthening without spinal compensation. Hip stabilization in non-weight-bearing position. Progression through sagittal (A), frontal (B), and rotational planes." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 67) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Aspirants must practice the breathing methods with attention while in the states of this asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1432) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Maintain a firm uddiyana bandha to keep the lower back pressed to the floor during the transition." 
*(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Bending:** "You must keep your leg straight as you lower it... Allowing it to bend during this action might lead to injury." *(B. K. S. Iyengar Yoga: The Path to Holistic Health, B. K. S. Iyengar, p. 243) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Pelvic Tilting:** "Do not allow the opposite (left) leg to tilt out or lift off the mat; this indicates a loss of pelvic stability." *(B. K. S. Iyengar Yoga: The Path to Holistic Health, B. K. S. Iyengar, p. 243) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "If the fingers do not reach the toe easily, a yoga belt is used." *(B. K. S. Iyengar Yoga: The Path to Holistic Health, B. K. S. Iyengar, p. 242) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Press your thigh into its socket. Press your right hip and waist down." *(B. K. S. Iyengar Yoga: The Path to Holistic Health, B. K. S. Iyengar, p. 243) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
