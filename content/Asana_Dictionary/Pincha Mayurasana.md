# Pincha Mayurasana (Feathered Peacock Pose)

![Pincha-Mayurasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Pincha-Mayurasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Pincha means a feather. Mayura means a peacock. This asana represents a peacock with its feathers spread." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 315) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Pincha Mayurasana has twelve vinyasas. The 7th is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1433) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Place the forearms on the floor, elbows as wide as the shoulders. Keep the palms flat. Exhale, kick up and balance on the forearms, keeping the legs straight and the body vertical." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 315) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"sapta 7: inhale, kick up into the forearm balance. Breathe five times." 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 61) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Nose (nasagre) - or between the thumbs.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Forearm balance, body vertical, legs together and straight.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Infraspinatus & Teres Minor:** "Crucial for stabilizing the shoulder joint in external rotation to prevent the elbows from splaying." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Serratus Anterior:** "Active to keep the shoulders from 'sinking' towards the floor." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Trapezius (Lower/Middle):** "Engage to stabilize the scapulae." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Shoulder flexion (intense). Forearm pronation. Spinal extension (controlled). Hip extension. Knee extension." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Mula Bandha and Uddiyana Bandha are the 'rudder' of this inversion. They prevent the lower back from 'banana-ing' and keep the center of gravity stable over the base of the forearms." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 809) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Shoulder Impingement:** "Lack of external rotation in the humerus can lead to the subacromial space closing, pinching the tendons." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 305) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Lumbar Hyperextension:** "If the core is weak, the weight falls into the lower back, creating an unstable arch." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 308) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Press the forearms firmly into the floor to 'lift' out of the shoulders." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Keep the gaze forward to help maintain the balance, but don't crunch the neck." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"This inversion is harder than Sirsasana because the base (forearms) is narrower and requires more shoulder mobility." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 316) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
