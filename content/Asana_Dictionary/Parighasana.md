# Parighasana (Gate Pose)

![Parighasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Parighasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Parigha means a beam or bar used for shutting a gate. In this posture, the body resembles a cross beam used for locking a gate." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 100) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Parighasana has five vinyasas. The 3rd is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1438) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Kneel on the floor. Stretch the right leg sideways to the right. Inhale, stretch the arms out. Exhale, bend the torso to the right and reach for the right foot with the right hand. Reach over with the left arm and hold the right foot with both hands." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 100–101) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Third eye (nasagre/midbrow)
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Kneeling on one knee, other leg extended to the side, lateral side stretch over the extended leg.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Intercostals:** "Stretched on the upper side and contracted on the lower side of the torso." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Quadratus Lumborum:** "Deep lateral stretch." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Obliques:** "Active to control the lateral descent." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Spinal lateral flexion. Hip abduction. Knee extension (extended leg). Knee flexion (kneeling leg)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Uddiyana Bandha is essential to prevent the torso from 'collapsing' forward. The lateral stretch must stay in the frontal plane." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 814) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Lumbar Shear:** "If the torso rotates forward, the lateral stretch becomes a forward fold, losing the benefit and potentially straining the lower back." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 355) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Knee Compression:** "The kneeling knee can become painful if the weight is not distributed through the extended leg." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 358) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Keep the chest open and facing forward, not looking at the floor." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Imagine you are between two panes of glass." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"This asana is excellent for people who feel 'stiff' in the sides of the body. it improves breathing by opening the ribcage." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 101) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
