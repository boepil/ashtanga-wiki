# Ubhaya Padangusthasana (Both Big Toes Pose)

![Ubhaya-Padangusthasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Ubhaya-Padangusthasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Ubhaya means both, padangustha means big toe." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 260) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"This asana has 15 vinyasas, of which the 9th is the state." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1434) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"In the 8th vinyasa... catch the big toes and lift the legs over the head (as in Supta Konasana). In the 9th vinyasa, roll forward and balance on the sit bones with the legs straight up... this is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1434) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Towards the sky
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Balance on the sit bones, hold the big toes, arms and legs are extended, head back, open chest.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Hip Flexors:** "Psoas and iliacus work intensely to maintain the V-shape while balancing." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Core Stabilizers:** "Transversus abdominis and obliques are essential for core integration to maintain the balance point." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 67) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Erector Spinae:** "Keep the spine straight and prevent the back from rounding." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Hip flexion (deep). Knee extension. Shoulder flexion and abduction (to reach the toes)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Engage root lock (Mula Bandha) to stabilize the center of gravity on the sit bones." 
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 106) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

"In the 9th vinyasa, as you roll up, inhale deeply and engage uddiyana bandha to facilitate the lift." 
*(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Sacral Pressure:** "Resting on the top of the sacrum, not on the sitting bones... puts a lot of stress into the fully flexed lower spine." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 81) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Hamstring Overstretch:** "Pulling the legs too aggressively while balancing can strain the hamstring attachments if the core is weak." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 83) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Keep the spine straight. If the legs cannot straighten, keep the knees slightly bent to protect the lower back." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 148) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Maintain the lift in the chest to prevent collapsing into the lumbar spine." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 150) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
