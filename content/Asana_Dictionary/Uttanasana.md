# Uttanasana

"Uttana means intense stretch. In this pose, the whole back of the body is intensely stretched."

![Uttanasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Uttanasana-copy-e1583305619445-200x300.png)

## Detail
Uttanasana is a standing forward fold where the torso hangs over the legs, and the hands reach toward the floor or the ankles.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Hamstring Lengthening**: Maximum stretch for the biceps femoris, semitendinosus, and semimembranosus.
- **Spinal Decompression**: Gravity assists in lengthening the spine, especially when the neck is relaxed.
- **Pelvic Tilt**: Success in Uttanasana depends on the ability of the pelvis to tilt forward (nutation) at the hip joints.
