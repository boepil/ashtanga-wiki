# Parivrtta Trikonasana

"Parivrtta means revolved, turned round or back. Trikona is a triangle. This is the revolving triangle posture. It is a counter pose to Utthita Trikonasana." 

![Utthita-Trikonasana-b](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Uthitha-Trikonasana-b.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 164) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Utthitatrikonasana A [and] B... 5 vinyasas." [Note: In the Ashtanga system, 'B' refers to the revolved version]. 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 631) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Stand in Tadasana. Take a deep inhalation and with a jump spread the legs apart sideways 3 to 3½ feet... Turn the right foot sideways 90 degrees to the right. Turn the left foot 60 degrees to the right... Exhale, rotate the trunk along with the left leg in the opposite direction (to the right) so as to bring the left palm on the floor near the outer side of the right foot." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 165) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Inhale, lift the left hand from the floor, rotate the trunk back to its original position... Exhale and jump back to Tadasana." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 166, 168) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Finger tip of the right hand
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Revolve around, bring left hand to the floor outside the right foot, right arm up. Repeat on the left side.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- "The internal and external oblique muscles activate to rotate the spine... Contraction of the internal oblique... accentuates twisting postures such as Parivrtta Trikonasana." *(Yoga Mat Companion 1, Ray Long) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "The pectineus contracts... assisting the iliopsoas, flexing the forward hip... This same principle applies in Parivrtta Trikonasana." *(Yoga Mat Companion 1, Ray Long) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Contracting the gluteus medius in the back leg of Parivrtta Trikonasana accentuates the twist from the trunk by rotating the femur." *(Yoga Mat Companion 1, Ray Long) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "The vertebral column undergoes a complex series of coupled movements, including rotation, flexion, and extension at various levels." *(The Manual of Yoga Anatomy, Sally Parkes) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "The pelvis has to tilt forwards. This is achieved by the hip flexors... while the iliopsoas stabilizes the pelvis." *(Yoga Mat Companion 1, Ray Long) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "In Parivrtta Trikonasana the shoulders rotate in one direction and the pelvis in another." *(Identifying Postural Imbalances, Vayu Jung Doohwa) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Rooting evenly through your feet while lifting your inner arches, spiral your inner thighs back while drawing your sitting bones toward each other and lifting steadily from the front floor of your pelvis with mula bandha." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 801) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"All through the pose, keep the pelvis directed forward." 
*(A Little Book of Yoga, Orit Sen-Gupta, p. 30) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- "Practitioners with lower back injuries and/or back conditions should practise this asana with caution." *(Science of Yoga, Ann Swanson) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "If the torso does not rotate fully to the right, keep the chin in line with the sternum to avoid neck injury." *(Science of Yoga, Ann Swanson) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "The asymmetrical nature and internal rotation of the back leg can put stress on the SI joint if not stabilized." *(Your Body, Your Yoga, Bernie Clark) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Lengthen Before You Twist: Inhaling, reach the left hand to the floor... now begin to exhale and rotate the torso from the navel." *(Science of Yoga, Ann Swanson) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "If the hand does not comfortably reach the floor, place a yoga brick underneath it. This will help maintain even length through either side of the torso." *(Science of Yoga, Ann Swanson) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Lever with the Arm: Press the hand into the side of the foot to lever the trunk into the twist." *(Yoga Mat Companion 1, Ray Long) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
