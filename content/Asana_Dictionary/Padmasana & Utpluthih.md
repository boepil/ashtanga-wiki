# Padmasana & Utpluthih

"Padma means a lotus. Padmasana is the Lotus Pose, the most important posture for meditation." 

![Padmasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Padmasana.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 104) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Utpluthi means a spring or a jump. Also known as Tolasana (Scale Pose)." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 792) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Padmasana is the 10th vinyasa of the final sequence. Utpluthih is the 11th." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1413) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Sit on the floor... bend the right leg and place the right foot on the base of the left thigh. Then bend the left leg and place the left foot on the base of the right thigh." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 104) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"daśa 10: inhale, sit in Padmasana and place the hands on the knees in Jñāna Mudra. Breathe ten times." 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 52) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"ekādaśa 11: inhale, lift the body off the floor. Breathe ten times [or more]." 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 53) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Tip of the nose
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
- **Padmasana:** Rest the hands on the knees in Chin Mudra (or Jñāna Mudra), shoulders away from the ears, spine straight.
- **Utpluthih:** Palms on the floor, lift the entire body off the floor using core and arm strength. Maintain the lift for as long as possible.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Serratus Anterior:** "In Utpluthih, this is the primary muscle responsible for depressing the scapulae and lifting the trunk." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Iliopsoas:** "Actively engaged in both poses to maintain the lift of the legs." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Triceps Brachii:** "Extend the arms in Utpluthih to support the weight of the body." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Hip external rotation and flexion. Knee flexion. Scapular depression (in Utpluthih). Spinal neutral." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Engage Mula Bandha, Uddiyana Bandha, and Jalandhara Bandha (Mahabandha) while in Padmasana." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 792) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"In Utpluthih, the root lock (Mula Bandha) is essential for the lift." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1413) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Injury:** "Applying too much force to the lotus position when the hips are tight causes the knee to act as a hinge in the wrong plane." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 150) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Wrist Compression:** "In Utpluthih, weight must be distributed across the entire palm and fingers to avoid compressing the carpal tunnel." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 152) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Use easier variations like Sukhasana (Easy Pose) or Ardha Padmasana (Half Lotus) if full lotus causes any knee discomfort." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Focus on the upward lift from the core rather than just pushing with the arms in Utpluthih." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
