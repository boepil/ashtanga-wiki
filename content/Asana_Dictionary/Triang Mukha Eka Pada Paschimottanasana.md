# Triang Mukha Eka Pada Paschimottanasana (Three-Limb Face One-Foot Seated Forward Bend)

![Triang-Mukha-ek-Pada-Paschimattanasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Triang-Mukha-ek-Pada-Paschimattanasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Trianga means three limbs or parts thereof. In this posture the three parts are the feet, knees and buttocks. Mukhaikapada (a compound of three words, mukha = face, eka = one, and pada = leg or foot) corresponds to the face (or mouth) touching one (extended) leg. In Paschimottanasana the back of the whole body is intensely stretched." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 254) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Tiriang Mukha Eka Pada Paschimottanasana: Three Limb Face One Foot Western Intense Stretch Pose." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 952) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Tiriangmukhaikapada Paschimattanasana has twenty-two vinyasas, with the 8th and 15th vinyasas constituting its states." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1513) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"At the 7th vinyasa, jump through the arms, sit as described in Paschimattanasana, stretch the left leg out, fold the right leg back, placing the right foot by the side of the thigh, join the knees together, take hold with both hands of the left foot... lift the head and chest up fully, and do puraka... Then, doing rechaka slowly, place the forehead on the outstretched leg... this is the 8th vinyasa." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1514) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Sit on the floor, with the legs stretched straight in front. ... Bend the right leg at the knee and move the right foot back. Place the right foot by the side of the right hip joint, keep the toes pointing back and rest them on the floor. The inner side of the right calf will touch the outer side of the right thigh." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 254) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Inhale, raise the head and trunk, release the hands, straighten the right leg... Repeat the pose on the other side." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 256) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Big toe of the extended leg
*(raw PDF)*

## Movement
Bend the right leg, place the foot by the hip, left leg extended to the front, fold forward, hold the right wrist with the left hand around the foot, bring the chin to the shin. Repeat on the left side.
*(raw PDF)*

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- "Folded leg: rectus femoris and quadriceps eccentric lengthening; Tests anterior hip/thigh flexibility." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 77) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Bending the knee [of the folded leg] stretches the vastus lateralis, medialis, and intermedius. The rectus femoris relaxes due to the flexed position of the hip." *(Key Muscles of Yoga, Ray Long, p. 243) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "The straight leg quadriceps contract, stretching the corresponding hamstrings [through reciprocal inhibition]." *(Key Muscles of Yoga, Ray Long, p. 243) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "The tendency is to lean to the straight-leg side. Balance this by engaging the muscles that push and draw you towards the bent-knee side." *(Yoga Mat Companion 1, Ray Long, p. 477) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "The spine is stretched over 'one foot' and the face rests on the leg. ... In the final stretch, make sure your body weight is distributed evenly over your legs and buttocks." *(B. K. S. Iyengar Yoga: The Path to Holistic Health, B. K. S. Iyengar, p. 133, 136) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "The pelvis tips forward, drawing rotation at the hip joint. This allows the spine to extend long over the legs." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 22) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Aspirants should not forget to do rechaka and puraka slowly and as much as possible, while in the state of this asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1515) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"In all seated forward bends, make the grounding of the sitting bones the primary action." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 890) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- "If the hip stops rotating... the torque transfers to the knee. This stresses the medial collateral ligament (MCL) and meniscus." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 24) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Do not twist your torso or allow it to lean towards the outer side of your extended leg, as this could strain your spine or abdominal organs." *(B. K. S. Iyengar Yoga: The Path to Holistic Health, B. K. S. Iyengar, p. 134) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "If hamstrings [of the straight leg] are tight, they pull the pelvis into Posterior Tilt... forcing the lumbar spine to round. This loads the intervertebral discs safely." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 22) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Use the hand to open the calf muscle of the bent leg to the side [to allow the sit bone to reach the floor]." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 119) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Ensure your thighs and knees are pressed together. Rest on both buttocks—the essence of the pose is getting this balance right." *(B. K. S. Iyengar Yoga: The Path to Holistic Health, B. K. S. Iyengar, p. 135) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "If the sit bone of the straight leg is lifting or if there is knee pain, sit on a block or blanket. This elevates the hips and allows the spine to extend vertically." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 26, 119) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- "Rule: Never force the knee. ... The knee should remain closed (flexed) to protect itself." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 24) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
