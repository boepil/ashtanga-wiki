# Karnapidasana (Ear Pressure Pose)

![Karnapidasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Karnapidasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Karṇa means the ear. Pīḍā means pain, discomfort or pressure. This is a variant of Halasana and can be done along with it." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 256) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"There are thirteen vinyasas in this asana and the 8th is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1405) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Do Halasana... flex the knees and rest the right knee by the side of the right ear and the left knee by the side of the left.... Both knees should rest on the floor." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 256) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"aṣṭau 8: exhale, bend the knees around the head and press them to the ears. Keep the heels touching and the tops of the feet pressing into the floor. Breathe eight times." 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 48) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Tip of the nose
*(raw PDF)*

## Movement
Bring the knees to the floor by the ears, press on the ears with the knees, feet together, arms on the floor, fingers interlaced.
*(raw PDF)*

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Inner Core:** "Uddiyana bandha is essential to prevent compression of the abdominal organs while the trunk is flexed." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Gluteus Maximus:** "Stretched as the hips move into extreme flexion." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Paraspinals:** "Intensely lengthened along the entire length of the spine." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Cervical flexion. Spinal flexion. Deep hip flexion. Knee flexion." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"In the state of this asana, the stomach should be drawn in fully without tightening the anus [Uddiyana Bandha], and rechaka and puraka performed." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1405) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Cervical Hyperflexion:** "The pressure of the knees against the floor can push the weight too far onto the neck if the shoulder foundation is weak." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 105) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Restricted Breathing:** "The compression of the chest and throat can lead to panic or shallow breathing if the student is not relaxed." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 108) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Keep the heels together and the tops of the feet pressing into the floor to maintain stability and prevent the knees from splaying." *(raw PDF)*
- "Rule: Focus on breathing into the back of the lungs to maintain internal space during the compression." *(raw PDF)*

## Teaching Notes
"This pose gives rest to the heart and the legs. It also helps to internalize the senses (Pratyahara) by blocking the ears." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 257) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
