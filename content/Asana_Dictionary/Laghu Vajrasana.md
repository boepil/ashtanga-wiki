# Laghu Vajrasana

"Laghu means small or little. Vajra means a thunderbolt (the weapon of Indra). Laghu Vajrasana is a variation of the Thunderbolt pose focusing on deep spinal extension." 

![Laghu-Vajrasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Laghu-Vajrasana.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 167) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Laghu Vajrasana has fifteen vinyasas. The 8th is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1424) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Kneel on the floor... place the palms on the knees. Exhale, arch the back and lower the crown of the head to the floor. Keep the thighs vertical." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 167) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"aṣṭau 8: inhale, arch back and place the head on the floor. Hands on knees. Breathe five times." 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 57) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Quadriceps:** "Powerful eccentric engagement is required to lower the body with control and to lift back up." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Deep Neck Flexors:** "Active to stabilize the head position when the crown touches the floor." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Psoas:** "Intense eccentric load during the descent." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Spinal extension (intense lumbar and thoracic). Hip extension. Knee flexion. Cervical extension." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Uddiyana Bandha must be maintained throughout the descent and ascent to protect the internal organs and provide the necessary core leverage to return to a kneeling position." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 800) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Strain:** "If the thighs are not kept vertical, the force of gravity pulls on the patellar tendon and can cause knee pain." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 215) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Cervical Compression:** "Collapsing into the head rather than maintaining the arch puts dangerous pressure on the neck." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 218) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Push the knees and shins firmly into the floor to help 'lift' the hips forward." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Only lower as far as you can while maintaining the ability to come back up with control." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"This asana prepares the practitioner for Kapotasana by strengthening the legs and deepening the spinal arch while keeping the foundation stable." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 168) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
