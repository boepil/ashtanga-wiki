# Bakasana (Crane Pose)

![Bakasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Bakasana-A.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Baka means a crane. In this posture, the body resembles a crane wading in a pool." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 308) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Variations
### Bakasana A
Squat on the floor, palms flat. Place the knees on the back of the upper arms, near the armpits. Exhale, lean forward and lift the feet off the floor. Straighten the arms as much as possible.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

### Bakasana B
From Adho Mukha Svanasana, jump forward and land with the knees directly in the armpits, balancing immediately.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Bakasana has twelve vinyasas. The 7th is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1427) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Squat... place the palms flat on the floor... place the knees on the back of the upper arms near the armpits. Exhale, lean forward and lift the toes off the floor... straighten the arms." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 308) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Nose (nasagre)
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Arm balance, knees in armpits, arms straight.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Serratus Anterior:** "Crucial for scapular protraction, which creates the 'lift' and prevents the shoulders from collapsing." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Triceps Brachii:** "Maintain elbow extension against the weight of the body." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Deep Core:** "Transversus abdominis and rectus abdominis engage to pull the knees tight to the chest." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Scapular protraction. Shoulder flexion. Elbow extension. Hip flexion (intense). Knee flexion." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Uddiyana Bandha is the engine of Bakasana. The lift is not just from the arms but from the deep abdominal suction that pulls the hips up." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 803) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Wrist Strain:** "Placing all the weight on the heels of the hands can lead to wrist impingement." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 245) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Shoulder Collapsing:** "Lack of serratus engagement leads to 'winging' scapulae and instability." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 248) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Spread the fingers wide and grip the floor with the fingertips to distribute weight." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Keep the gaze slightly forward rather than straight down to prevent falling forward on the head." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"Bakasana strengthens the arms and abdominal organs and improves balance and coordination." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 309) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
