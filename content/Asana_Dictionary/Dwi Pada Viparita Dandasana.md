# Dwi Pada Viparita Dandasana

"Dwi means two, Pada means foot, Viparita means inverted, and Danda means a staff. This is the Two-Legged Inverted Staff Pose."

![Dwi Pada Viparita Dandasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 15) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A deep backbend performed from an inverted position (like a headstand or wheel), where the forearms are on the floor and the legs are extended.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Spinal Extension**: "Maximum spinal extension and shoulder flexor lengthening." [17, 18]
- **Shoulder Flexion**: Requires extreme openness in the latissimus dorsi and pectoralis major to keep the forearms grounded while the chest opens.
- **Nervous System Stimulation**: "Stimulates the central nervous system to increase stress tolerance." [17]
