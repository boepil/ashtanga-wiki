# Chakorasana

"Cakora is a bird that lives on moonbeams. This advanced arm balance requires one leg to be tucked behind the head while the other is extended forward."

![Chakorasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 9) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
Chakorasana is an arm balance where the practitioner lifts the body off the floor with one leg behind the head and the other leg extended straight out in front.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Triceps and Serratus Anterior**: Provide the necessary lift and stabilization for the arm balance.
- **Deep Core (Bandhas)**: Critical for maintaining the lift and stabilizing the spine against the weight of the legs.
- **Hip Flexibility**: Requires mastery of external rotation to keep the leg securely behind the head without straining the neck.
