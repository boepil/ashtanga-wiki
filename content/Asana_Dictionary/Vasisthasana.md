# Vasisthasana

"Vasiṣṭha is the name of a famous sage. Vasiṣṭhāsana is a side plank pose that integrates the strength of the arms with the lateral stability of the core."

![Vasisthasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Vasisthasana-copy-e1583306153439-279x300.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 2) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
The pose is a side plank performed on one hand with the top leg lifted and the hand holding the big toe. It requires significant balance and triceps strength.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Obliques**: "Excellent for strengthening the internal and external obliques and stabilizers, crucial for lateral stability." [6]
- **Shoulder Girdle**: Kinetic forces traveling along the arm and hand stimulate circulation through the heart, lung, and pericardium meridians. [8]
- **Triceps & Serratus Anterior**: Provide the necessary support to keep the humerus integrated in the socket during weight-bearing. [14]

## Vinyasa Entry
From Downward Facing Dog, shift weight to the right hand and the outer edge of the right foot. Lift the left leg and catch the big toe.
