# Ustrasana (Camel Pose)

![Ustrasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Ustrasana.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
"Ustra means a camel. In this posture, the body resembles a camel." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 165) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Ustrasana has seven vinyasas. The 3rd is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1423) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Kneel on the floor with the knees and feet together. Rest on the shins. Place the palms on the hips. Stretch the thighs, arch the back and push the hips forward. Exhale, place the right palm over the right heel and the left palm over the left heel. If possible, place the palms on the soles of the feet." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 165) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Nose (nasagre)
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Kneeling backbend, hands to heels.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Quadriceps:** "Active eccentric engagement to prevent the hips from falling back." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Iliopsoas:** "Intensely stretched as the hips are pushed forward into extension." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Sternocleidomastoid:** "Stretched as the head is dropped back (in the traditional version)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Spinal extension (thoracic and lumbar). Hip extension. Knee flexion. Shoulder extension and external rotation." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Mula Bandha is critical to stabilize the pelvic floor and provide an upward 'lift' that counters the deep compression of the lumbar spine." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 799) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Cervical Hyperextension:** "Dropping the head back without control can pinch the cervical nerves." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 205) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Lumbar Collapse:** "If the hips move behind the knees, the weight falls into the lower back rather than being distributed through the legs." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 208) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Keep the hips directly over the knees throughout the pose." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Lift the chest towards the ceiling to maximize thoracic extension before reaching for the heels." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"This asana is beneficial for people with drooping shoulders and a hunched back. The entire spine is stretched and toned." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 166) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
