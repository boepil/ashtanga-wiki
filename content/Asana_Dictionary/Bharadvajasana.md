# Bharadvajasana

"Bharadvaja was the father of Drona, the teacher of the Pandavas. This asana is dedicated to him." 

![Bharadvajasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Bharadvajasana-e1583309038788-194x300.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 254) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Bharadvajasana has five vinyasas. The 3rd is the state of the asana." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1428) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Sit on the floor... bend the knees and move the feet back and to the right side... rest the buttocks on the floor. Turn the trunk 90 degrees to the left. Straighten the right arm and place the right palm on the outer side of the left thigh. Reach back with the left arm and hold the right upper arm near the elbow." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 254–255) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"In the Ashtanga variation, one leg is often in Padmasana (Half Lotus)." 
*(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Side (parshva drishti) - looking over the shoulder.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Seated twist, legs folded to one side (or one in half-lotus), arm binding behind the back.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Obliques:** "Primary drivers of the spinal rotation." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Erector Spinae:** "Keep the spine upright during the twist to avoid compression." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Rhomboids:** "Active in the binding arm to pull the shoulder back." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Spinal rotation. Hip external rotation and flexion. Shoulder internal rotation and retraction (in the bind)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Uddiyana Bandha is essential during the twist to protect the lumbar spine and allow for deeper rotation in the thoracic region." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 804) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Strain:** "If performing the half-lotus variation, forcing the foot without hip mobility can torque the knee." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 255) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Slumping:** "Twisting with a rounded spine can lead to disc herniation over time." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 258) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "Inhale to find length in the spine before exhaling to rotate." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Keep both sit-bones grounded to ensure the twist is even." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
"This twist is considered more 'accessible' than Pasasana but serves as a deep spinal cleanser and preparation for Ardha Matsyendrasana." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 256) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*
