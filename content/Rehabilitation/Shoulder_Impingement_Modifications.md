## Overview
The objective during rehabilitation is to maintain a consistent practice without aggravating the subacromial space. This involves reducing load on the shoulder joint, restoring proper scapular mechanics, and avoiding poses that force the humeral head into the acromion.

**General Principle:** Reduce load (knees down), restore mechanics (protraction), and favor functional alignment over aesthetic depth.

## Poses to Avoid Temporarily
- **Full [[Surya_Namaskar]] (Chaturanga):** High risk of "dipping" the shoulders, which compresses the supraspinatus.
- **Full [[Urdhva Dhanurasana]]:** Deep overhead extension can pinch the shoulder joint if thoracic mobility is limited.
- **[[Pincha Mayurasana]] / (Handstand):** Extreme weight-bearing in overhead ranges before stability is restored.
- **[[Purvottanasana]]:** Puts the biceps tendon in a deep, potentially vulnerable stretch under load.

## Poses to Emphasize
- **[[Prasarita Padottanasana]] (C):** Gentle opening of the shoulders without weight-bearing (if pain-free).
- **[[Surya_Namaskar]] (Downward Dog):** Focus on wide hands and external rotation.
- **[[Surya_Namaskar]] (Cobra):** Encourages thoracic extension and lower trap engagement without the full weight-bearing of Upward Dog.

## Key Modifications by Pose

### 1. [[Surya_Namaskar]] (Downward Dog)
- **Change:** Widen the hands slightly (to the edges of the mat) and turn the index fingers out.
- **Cue:** "Spin the triceps toward the floor and hollow the armpits." This encourages external rotation.
- **Return:** Reintroduce shoulder-width hands when clicking/pinching disappears.

### 2. [[Surya_Namaskar]] (Chaturanga)
- **Change:** Always drop the knees to the floor. Do not lower the chest below the level of the elbows.
- **Cue:** "Broaden the collarbones and push the floor away." Maintain protraction.
- **Return:** Reintroduce knees-up when 15 slow, controlled reps can be done pain-free off-mat.

### 3. [[Urdhva Dhanurasana]] (Wheel)
- **Change:** Perform **Setu Bandhasana** (Bridge) or use blocks under the hands against a wall to reduce the angle of shoulder flexion.
- **Cue:** "Engage the inner thighs and lift from the tailbone."
- **Return:** Reintroduce full wheel when overhead range is restored and pain-free.

## Practice Structure During Rehabilitation
- **Mysore Style:** Ideal for recovery as it allows for individual pacing and skipping problematic vinyasas. Use "half-vinyasas" (skip the Chaturanga/Upward Dog and go straight to Down Dog) as needed.
- **Led Class:** Exercise caution. Do not feel pressured to keep the pace of the class if it compromises shoulder stability.

## Return to Full Practice
The compensation is resolved when the practitioner can maintain **Scapular Protraction** in a full Plank for 60 seconds and perform a full vinyasa without shrugging or "dipping" the shoulders.
