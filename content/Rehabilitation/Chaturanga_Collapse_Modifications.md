# Chaturanga collapse — asana modifications

## Overview
The goal is to prevent the repetitive "micro-trauma" caused by collapsing into the shoulder joints and lower back during the vinyasa. We shift the focus from "doing the vinyasa" to "building the strength for the vinyasa."

**General Principle:** Reduce the leverage and weight-bearing load to a level where the **Serratus Anterior** and **Deep Core** can maintain structural integrity.

## Poses to Avoid Temporarily
- **Full Jump-backs:** The explosive nature of the jump-back makes it difficult to maintain scapular protraction and core tension.
- **Full [[Surya_Namaskar]] (Chaturanga):** If the shoulders dip or the belly sags, the full version is reinforcing the compensation.
- **Extreme Arm Balances:** Poses like **Bakasana** (Crow) or **Lolasana** should be minimized if they result in significant "winging" of the scapulae.

## Poses to Emphasize
- **Modified [[Surya_Namaskar]] (Chaturanga - Knees Down):** To master the 90-degree alignment.
- **Phalakasana (Plank):** Holding the top of the plank with a "dome" in the upper back.
- **[[Surya_Namaskar]] (Upward Dog):** Focus on the downward rotation and depression of the scapula to create height and space for the neck.

## Key Modifications by Pose

### 1. [[Surya_Namaskar]] (Chaturanga)
- **Change:** Drop the knees to the floor before lowering. Place two blocks vertically under the shoulders to prevent them from dipping below elbow height.
- **Cue:** "Press the floor away and keep the elbows hugging the ribs."
- **Return:** Reintroduce knees-up once the "dome" in the upper back can be held for 15 seconds in the low position.

### 2. Vinyasa Jump-backs
- **Change:** Step back to Plank one foot at a time. Lower to Chaturanga with knees down.
- **Cue:** "Stay 'hollow' in the belly as you step back."
- **Return:** Gradually reintroduce the jump-back as the core strength and scapular stability improve.

### 3. [[Surya_Namaskar]] (Upward Dog)
- **Change:** If the shoulders are shrugging, stay in a low **Bhujangasana** (Cobra) with elbows bent and focus on drawing the shoulder blades together and down.
- **Cue:** "Lift the chest and roll the shoulders back."
- **Return:** Reintroduce full Upward Dog when the arms can be fully straightened without the shoulders creeping toward the ears.

## Practice Structure During Rehabilitation
- **Vinyasa Reduction:** Skip every second vinyasa or skip the Chaturanga/Upward Dog entirely, moving directly from Plank to Downward Dog.
- **Focus on Foundations:** Use the Standing Sequence to build the necessary core and leg strength to support the weight of the torso in arm balances.

## Return to Full Practice
Resolution is reached when the practitioner can transition from Plank to Chaturanga and into Upward Dog without the medial borders of the scapulae lifting ("winging") and without the hips sinking toward the mat.
