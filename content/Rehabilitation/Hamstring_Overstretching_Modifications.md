# Hamstring Overstretching Modifications

## Overview
The goal during rehabilitation from "yoga butt" (proximal hamstring tendinopathy) is to remove the "yanking" force on the sit-bone while keeping the muscle active. We move away from passive, gravity-led stretching and toward **Active, Resistance-based Lengthening**.

**General Principle:** Micro-bend the knees in all forward folds. If you feel a sharp pinch at the sit-bone, you have gone too far.

## Poses to Avoid Temporarily
- **Deep Passive Forward Folds ([[Paschimottanasana]]):** Especially those where the practitioner "pulls" themselves into the shape using the arms.
- **[[Supta Kurmasana]]:** The combined deep hip flexion and external rotation puts maximal stress on the proximal hamstring attachments.
- **Extreme Splits (Hanumanasana):** The explosive eccentric load is too high for a recovering tendon.

## Poses to Emphasize
- **[[Utkatasana]] (Chair Pose):** Builds the necessary strength to support the hamstrings.
- **[[Setu Bandhasana]] (Bridge):** Symmetrical posterior chain engagement.
- **[[Surya_Namaskar]] (Modified Down Dog):** Keeping the knees bent to prioritize spinal length and pelvic neutral.

## Key Modifications by Pose

### 1. [[Paschimottanasana]] (Seated Forward Fold)
- **Change:** Bend the knees significantly. Dig the heels into the mat as if trying to "scrub" the mat backward. Keep the spine straight rather than rounding.
- **Cue:** "Press the heels down and pull the sit-bones back. Focus on the 'load' in the muscle, not the 'stretch' at the bone."
- **Return:** Gradually straighten the legs only when the "pinch" at the sit-bone is absent for 3 consecutive practices.

### 2. [[Pada Angusthasana]] & [[Pada Hastasana]]
- **Change:** Bend the knees until the belly touches the thighs. This ensures the hinge is happening at the hips and not by "hanging" on the hamstring tendons.
- **Cue:** "Keep the contact between belly and thighs as you slowly lift the sit-bones."
- **Return:** Deepen the fold only when the eccentric control is established.

### 3. [[Supta Padangusthasana]] (Reclining Hand-to-Toe)
- **Change:** Use a strap. Keep the leg at a 90-degree angle (or lower) and focus on "pressing the leg into the strap" to create an isometric contraction.
- **Cue:** "Press the leg away from you while holding the strap. Create resistance."
- **Return:** Pull the leg closer to the head only when the tendon can handle the increased angle of flexion without pain.

## Practice Structure During Rehabilitation
- **Vinyasa Modification:** Step back to Plank instead of jumping back to avoid the sudden eccentric "jolt" to the hamstrings.
- **Active Engagement:** In every pose, focus on the "co-contraction" of the quads and glutes to support the joint.

## Return to Full Practice
The condition is resolved when the practitioner can perform a full **Paschimottanasana** with straight legs and active heels without any localized pain at the ischial tuberosity, and when the "morning stiffness" at the sit-bone has ceased.
