## Foundational Principles
- **The 70% Rule**: In the early stages of rehabilitation, load should not exceed 70% of the maximum comfortable range.
- **Progressive Loading**: Tendons and ligaments require controlled tension (loading) to heal, not just passive stretching.
- **WSM? (What Stops Me?)**: Constantly checking if a limitation is due to tension (muscular) or compression (skeletal) to avoid injury.
