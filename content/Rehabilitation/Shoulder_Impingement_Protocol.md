# Shoulder Impingement Protocol

This protocol addresses subacromial impingement by restoring the scapulohumeral rhythm and strengthening the stabilizing muscles of the rotator cuff and serratus anterior. As noted by Thomas Myers, the scapula must move the glenoid socket to "keep the peace" between the clavicle and humerus (Myers, Anatomy Trains, p. 494). A deficit in upward rotation, often caused by over-depressing the shoulder blades (Kaminoff, Yoga Anatomy, p. 83), reduces the subacromial space, leading to pain during overhead movements and pressing.

## Overview

## Guidelines
- Frequency: 3-5 days per week.
- Session time: 30-40 minutes.
- Principle: Pain-free range only. Focus on creating external rotation torque ("armpits forward") and maintaining a neutral spine (Starrett, Becoming a Supple Leopard, p. 183).

## Pain guide
- 0: No pain — good to train
- 1-3: Mild — OK with caution, reduce range if needed
- 4+: Sharp — stop and modify immediately

## Phase A — Prep / Mobility (5-8 min)

### Serratus Smash
(Starrett, Becoming a Supple Leopard, p. 340-342)
- How: Lie on a lacrosse ball positioned between the lat and pec, just under the armpit. Outstretch the arm and "scrub" across the tissue with slow movements.
- Duration: 2 minutes per side.
- Target: Releases stiff serratus and intercostal tissues to improve scapular protraction.

### Shoulder Capsule Mobilization
(Starrett, Becoming a Supple Leopard, p. 336)
- How: Use a heavy kettlebell (floor press position) to drive the humerus to the back of the socket while performing internal and external rotation.
- Duration: 2 minutes per side.
- Target: Resets the humerus into a stable posterior position and stretches the posterior capsule.

### Modified Bully Stretch
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 375)
- How: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, clasp hands behind the back. Pinch shoulder blades together, raise the chest, and gently pull the arms down.
- Sets/Reps: 3 holds of 30 seconds.
- Target: Depresses the scapula and stretches the anterior shoulder capsule.

## Phase B — Strength (20-25 min)

### Band Facepull
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 381-382)
- How: Anchor a band above eye level. Pull handles toward ears while externally rotating. Use a neutral or palms-up grip.
- Sets/Reps: 3 sets of 15-20 reps.
- Target: "Magic bullet" for shoulder pain; builds upper back endurance and posterior rotator cuff strength.

### Banded W
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 379)
- How: Hold a light resistance band and pull into a "W" shape, isolating external rotators.
- Sets/Reps: 3 sets of 12-15 reps.
- Target: End-range motion control and stability for the external rotators.

### Scapular Pull-Up (Active Bar Hanging)
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 371-372)
- How: Hang from a bar with straight elbows. Pull the scapulae back and down (active hang), then relax.
- Sets/Reps: 3 sets of 8-12 reps.
- Target: Prepares the scapula for vertical pulling and decompressing the spine.

### Band Push-Up Plus
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 363-364)
- How: Perform a push-up; at the top, protract the scapulae to lift the upper back as high as possible.
- Sets/Reps: 3 sets of 10-15 reps.
- Target: Serratus anterior activation to prevent impingement during pushing movements like Chaturanga.

## Phase C — Control / Endurance (5-10 min)

### Swimmer’s Stretch
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 376-377)
- How: Lying prone, move arms from overhead (thumbs up) to a T-shape, then into internal rotation with hands behind the low back.
- Sets/Reps: 3 sets of 5 slow reps.
- Target: Reinforces optimal scapular position and activates upper back endurance.

### Scapular Plane Raise (Scaption)
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 382)
- How: Raise dumbbells at a 30-degree angle in front of the frontal plane (not directly to the side).
- Sets/Reps: 3 sets of 12 reps.
- Target: Maintains subacromial space more effectively than traditional raises, preventing impingement.

### Strict Press (Light/Rehab Focus)
(Starrett, Becoming a Supple Leopard, p. 258)
- How: Press a light weight overhead while maintaining "armpits forward" and squeezed glutes/tight belly.
- Sets/Reps: 2 sets of 10 reps.
- Target: Functional integration of a stable shoulder position in an overhead pressing pattern.

## Progression
(Hogan, Built From Broken, p. 18)
Criteria for advancing through the protocol:
- **Week 1 (Remodeling):** 2 sets x 7 reps, Tempo 5s down / 5s up.
- **Week 2 (Hypertrophy):** 3 sets x 8-12 reps, Tempo 3s down / 3s up.
- **Week 3 (Strength):** 3 sets x 5-7 reps, Tempo 3s down / 1s up.
- **Week 4 (Endurance):** 3 sets x 15 reps, Tempo 1s down / 1s up.

## When to seek help
- Sharp, stabbing pain that persists at rest.
- Sensation of the shoulder "slipping" or significant instability.
- Inability to lift the arm above 90 degrees due to weakness (not just pain).
- Numbness or tingling radiating down the arm.
