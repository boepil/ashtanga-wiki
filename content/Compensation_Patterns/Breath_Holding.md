# Breath Holding

**Lack**  

![[ChatGPT Image May 6, 2026, 08_14_33 PM.png]]

### 1. Vinyasa Synchronization and Subtle Support

Lack of core/energetic support via the *Bandhas* and a failure to synchronize movement with the rhythm of the *Ujjayi* breath.

**Compensation**  
Unintentional *Kumbhaka* (breath holding) to create temporary, brittle stability via internal pressure; jaw clenching; facial strain; and "rushing" the physical movement to escape the lack of air.

**Where It Shows Up**  
- **Movement:** Lifting heavy objects, explosive athletic movements.  
- **Yoga:** Vinyasa transitions (jumping back/forward), lift-offs into arm balances, and the transition from **Chaturanga** to **Upward Dog** (see [[Surya_Namaskar]]).

**What to Train**  
- **Ujjayi Continuation:** Maintaining the sound and depth of the breath *during* the transition, not just in the pose.
- **Bandha Awareness:** Using [[Mula_Bandha]] and [[Uddiyana_Bandha]] to provide the necessary "lightness" and internal stability, reducing the need for the "airbag effect" of holding the breath.
- **Tempo Control:** Slowing down the transition to ensure the breath initiates and sustains the movement.

**Yoga Expression**  
A practice that feels "herky-jerky" or aggressive; physical exhaustion; and a loss of the "meditation in motion" quality. This is **Gross Effort** (muscular/pressure-based) replacing **Subtle Support** (energetic/breath-based).

**Meta Insight**  
"Gross pressure (holding breath) replacing subtle support (Bandhas)." The system uses the most primitive form of stabilization (glottal closure/IAP) when the more refined energetic locks are unavailable.

---

## Diagnostic Cue
If you find yourself exhaling sharply *after* a jump-back or a transition, you were likely holding your breath *during* the movement. Can you keep the Ujjayi sound perfectly steady from the moment your hands touch the mat until you land in Chaturanga?

## Common Misinterpretation
Practitioners often think that "holding the core" means "holding the breath." In reality, true [[Uddiyana_Bandha]] is a dynamic state of the lower abdomen that *allows* for the free movement of the diaphragm and the continuation of the breath.
