# Primary Series

## Summary
The Primary Series, known as *Yoga Chikitsa* (Yoga Therapy), is the foundational sequence in Ashtanga Yoga. It is designed to heal, detoxify, and align the physical body, preparing it for deeper practice. The series emphasizes forward folds, hip openers, and building core strength and stamina.

## Detail
The Primary Series follows a strict vinyasa count, linking breath with movement. It systematically works through the body to clear energy channels (nadis), generate internal heat, and purify the nervous system.

The structure of the Primary Series is:
1. **[[Surya_Namaskar]] A & B**: The opening salutations to generate heat.
2. **[[Standing_Sequence]]**: Fundamental postures that build grounding, balance, and leg strength.
3. **[[Seated_Sequence]]**: The core of Yoga Chikitsa, featuring intense forward folds, hip openers (like Marichyasana series), and core-strengthening poses (like Navasana).
4. **[[Finishing_Sequence]]**: Backbends, inversions, and deep relaxation to calm the nervous system and integrate the practice.

The Vinyasa system requires a continuous flow of movement between seated postures, jumping back to Chaturanga Dandasana, Upward Dog, and Downward Dog, which maintains internal heat and focus.
