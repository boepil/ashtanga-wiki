# Daily Sequence Notes

In Ashtanga Yoga, the sequence of postures is fixed. This strict constancy acts as a reliable mirror: because the sequence does not change, any variation you experience—whether a pose feels "easy" or "impossible"—is revealed to be a product of your own internal fluctuations (*Parinamavada*).

By meeting the same "peak" asanas every day, you recognize your habitual reactions and cultivate detachment (*Vairagya*).

### 1. Specific Posture Focus & Observations
Choose 1–3 asanas where you noticed a shift or significant resistance. Focus on *sthira* (stability) and *sukham* (ease).
- **Asana:** ________________ 
- **Observations:** (e.g., Grounding, space in lower back, or identifying "right pain" vs. injury warnings).

### 2. Breath Stability (Ujjāyī)
- **Consistency:** Was the Ujjayi sound steady and rhythmic, or did it become labored during transitions?
- **Barometer:** Did a shortened breath indicate "forcing" a pose rather than "playing the edge"?

### 3. Post-Practice State (Gunas)
Identify the predominant energetic quality after *Savasana*:
- **Sattvic:** Clear, light, tranquil, and focused.
- **Rajasic:** Agitated, hyper-intense, competitive, or restless.
- **Tamasic:** Heavy, dull, exhausted, or lethargic.

**Key Insight of the Day:** 
*(e.g., Noticed frustration arising in backbends; felt deeply grounded and Sattvic after standing poses).*
