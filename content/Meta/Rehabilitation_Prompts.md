### 6. Nervous System Dysregulation

- "Breathe Light to Breathe Right" (Reduce breathing volume by 20-30% to create a "tolerable air hunger." Maintain for 5 minutes.)

3 sets each, illustrated.

Bottom footer:
- Frequency: Daily. | Session: 10-20 minutes. | Pain guide: 0: Calm — good to train. | 1-3: Air Hunger — tolerable, keep inhalation calm. | 4+: Panic/Gasping — stop, the air shortage is too intense (McKeown, p. 145).
- Key Takeaway: "Strict nasal breathing at all times (day and night). Use "air hunger" as a signal for adaptation, not stress (Nestor, Breath, p. 251)."
