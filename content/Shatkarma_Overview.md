# Shatkarma Overview

The *Shatkarmas* (Six Cleansing Actions) are a set of traditional purificatory duties (*kriyas*) described in classical Hatha Yoga texts. They are designed to cleanse the physical body's internal environment, balance the bodily humors (*doshas*), and prepare the practitioner for advanced breathing (Pranayama) and meditation.

## The Six Cleansing Actions

1. **Dhauti**: Internal cleansing of the digestive tract (stomach and esophagus). Methods involve swallowing water, air, or traditionally, a moist strip of cloth to remove excess mucus.
2. **Basti**: A yogic enema. Traditionally performed by squatting in water and using abdominal vacuums to draw water into the colon for cleansing.
3. **[[Jala_Neti|Neti]]**: Nasal irrigation to clear the respiratory passages and "clean the brain."
4. **[[Trataka]]**: Concentrated gazing at a fixed point to cleanse the visual organs and steady the mind.
5. **Nauli**: Abdominal massage via the isolation and rotation of the *rectus abdominis* muscles to stimulate digestion and remove abdominal imbalances.
6. **[[Kapalabhati]]**: The "skull-shining" breath; a rapid, bellows-like breathing technique that dries up disorders caused by excess phlegm (*kapha*).

## Purpose and Philosophy

The central aim of these actions is to remove gross physical and energetic impurities.
- **Balancing the Doshas**: Traditional masters like Swami Swatmarama emphasized that these practices are crucial for anyone whose *doshas* (Kapha, Pitta, Vata) are not in perfect balance—specifically those with an excess of fat or phlegm.
- **Preparing the Nadis**: By clearing physiological obstructions (like mucus), the Shatkarmas ensure the proper flow of breath (*vayu*) through the energy channels (*nadis*).

## Hatha Kriya vs. Patanjali's Kriya

It is important to distinguish the somatic Kriya Yoga of the Hatha tradition from the psychological Kriya Yoga of Patanjali.
- **Somatic Kriya (Hatha):** Refers to the physical actions (the Shatkarmas) used to literally "bake" and cleanse the physical body.
- **Patanjali's Kriya:** Focuses on *Tapas* (discipline), *Svadhyaya* (self-study), and *Ishvara Pranidhana* (surrender to the divine).
