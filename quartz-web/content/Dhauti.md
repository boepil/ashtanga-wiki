# Dhauti (Internal Cleansing)

**Dhauti** is a [shatkarma](/Shatkarma_Overview) (purification) aimed at cleansing the digestive tract from mouth to rectum. The *Gheranda Samhita* divides it into four categories: Antara (internal), Danta (dental), Hrida (chest), and Mula Shodhana (rectal).

## Antara Dhauti (Internal)

Four methods:

- **Vatasara Dhauti** — Swallowing air to expand the stomach, then expelling it through the intestines with the aid of an inverted asana. The *Gheranda Samhita* cautions that this requires the ability to hold the breath for 90 minutes.
- **Varisara Dhauti** — Drinking warm salty water and performing a sequence of asanas until water flows from the anus. Requires expert supervision and a specific post-practice diet. A major cleansing operation.
- **Agnisara Dhauti** (Vahnisura Dhauti) — Rhythmic pumping of the abdomen in Vajrasana to generate internal heat (*agni*). Related to Agni Sara and Uddiyana Bandha.
- **Bahishkrita Dhauti** — Standing waist-deep in water, everting the rectum and washing it. Described as a "very difficult practice."

## Danta Dhauti (Dental)

- **Danta Mula** — Cleaning the teeth (traditionally with a neem stick)
- **Jihva Mula** — Cleaning the tongue
- **Kapalarandhra** — Cleaning the back of the soft palate
- **Karna Dhauti** — Cleaning the ears
- **Chaksu Dhauti** — Bathing the eyes (sometimes included)

## Hrida Dhauti (Chest)

- **Danda Dhauti** — Passing a soft stick (traditionally banana stem core) into the esophagus. Expert supervision required.
- **Vaman Dhauti** — Induced vomiting, e.g. with salty water and tickling the throat, performed three hours after a meal.
- **Vastra Dhauti** — Swallowing a long strip of thin cloth and removing it to clean the esophagus and stomach.

## Mula Shodhana (Rectal)

Washing the rectum using water and a finger or a turmeric stick. Related to [[Basti]].

## Benefits

- **Respiratory and mucus control** — Removes excess mucus and phlegm, alleviates respiratory disorders above the collarbone
- **Digestive and gastric health** — Cleanses the stomach, removes gastric impurities, and "destroys" stomach-related diseases
- **Stokes Jathara Agni** (digestive fire) — Techniques like Agni Sara fan the internal fire for metabolizing energy and "baking away" toxins (Ama)
- **Dosha balance** — Brings Kapha, Pitta, and Vata into equilibrium; especially indicated for excess fat or phlegm (Kapha)
- **Prepares for higher yoga** — Purifies the vessel for advanced Pranayama and meditation

## Contraindications

> [!CAUTION]
> Dhauti should **only** be performed under the direct supervision of an experienced teacher. Attempting Vastra, Danda, or Varisara Dhauti without guidance is dangerous. Contraindicated in: stomach ulcers, gastritis, severe esophageal sensitivity, hiatal hernia, active abdominal inflammation, pregnancy, hypertension, heart disease, recent abdominal surgery, poor lung capacity, or general physical debility.
