# Skandasana

"Skanda is the name of a sage, a son of Shiva and the god of war. This pose is a deep lunge with one leg behind the head."

![Skandasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 9) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A deep forward-folding lunge where one leg is tucked behind the head.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Hip Adductor & Hamstring Lengthening**: Intense stretch for the inner thigh and back of the leg.
- **External Rotation**: Critical for placing the leg safely behind the head without torque in the knee.
- **Spinal Flexion**: Requires significant length in the posterior chain.

## Connections
- [[Eka Pada Sirsasana & Dwi Pada Sirsasana]] — Foundational flexibility.
- [[Durvasasana]] — The standing version of this leg placement.
