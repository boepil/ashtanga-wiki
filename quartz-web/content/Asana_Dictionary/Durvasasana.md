# Durvasasana

"Durvāsā was a famous irascible sage. This pose is a standing balance performed with one leg behind the head and hands in prayer."

![Durvasasana](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Untitled-design-9-e1583821052402.png)

## Sanskrit Meaning
*(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, p. 9) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Detail
A challenging standing balance that requires the practitioner to stand on one leg while the other is behind the head, with hands in Anjali Mudra.

## Anatomical Focus
- **Single-Leg Stability**: Maximum demand on the hip stabilizers (Gluteus Medius/Minimus) and the standing foot/ankle.
- **External Hip Rotation**: Intense external rotation required to maintain the leg behind the head while upright.
- **Core Integration**: Essential for maintaining balance and preventing the torso from collapsing forward.
