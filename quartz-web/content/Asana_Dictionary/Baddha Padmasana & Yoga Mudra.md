# Baddha Padmasana & Yoga Mudra

"Baddha means bound. Padma means a lotus. Baddha Padmasana is the Locked Lotus Pose." 

![Yoga-Mudra-A](https://www.devvratyoga.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Yoga-Mudra-A.png)


## Sanskrit Meaning
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 119) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Yoga Mudra: The seal of Yoga. Often performed from Baddha Padmasana." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 791) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Vinyasa Count & Entry
"Baddha Padmasana has sixteen vinyasas, of which the 9th is the state." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1412) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"Sit in Padmasana. Bring the left arm behind the back from the shoulder and catch the left big toe. Then bring the right arm behind the back and catch the right big toe." 
*(Light on Yoga, B.K.S. Iyengar, p. 119) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"nava 9: (Yoga Mudra) exhale, fold forward and touch the forehead or chin to the floor. Breathe ten times." 
*(Ashtanga Yoga Anusthana, Sharath Jois, p. 52) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Drishti
Third eye
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Movement
Legs in Padmasana, wrap the arms crossed behind the back to grab the big toes with the opposite hand. For **Yoga Mudra**, exhale and forward fold until the forehead or chin touches the floor.
<div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Anatomical Focus
**Primary Muscles Engaged:**
- **Shoulder Girdle:** "Infraspinatus and subscapularis engage to facilitate the deep internal rotation and extension required for the bind." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Hip Rotators:** "Extreme external rotation of the femurs is required to bring the feet high onto the thighs for the bind." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Abdominals:** "Engage to facilitate the forward fold in Yoga Mudra." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Joint Actions:**
- "Shoulder internal rotation and extension. Hip external rotation and flexion. Knee flexion. Spinal flexion (in Yoga Mudra)." *(NotebookLM extraction) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

## Bandha Application
"Mula Bandha and Uddiyana Bandha are essential to create the space in the pelvic bowl for the feet to sit high enough for the bind." 
*(Teaching Yoga Essential Foundations and Techniques, Mark Stephens, p. 791) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

"The stomach should be drawn in fully during the forward fold." 
*(Yoga Mala, K. Pattabhi Jois, p. 1412) — Yoga Philosophy and Origins*

## Common Mistakes & Injury Flags
**Anatomical Risks:**
- **Knee Torque:** "Forcing the feet into position without sufficient hip rotation can cause the knee joint to rotate, risking meniscus or ligament tears." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 140) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*
- **Shoulder Impingement:** "Aggressive binding without proper shoulder mobility can lead to impingement of the rotator cuff." *(ASHTANGA_YOGA_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.pdf, Unknown, p. 142) — Yoga and Human Anatomy*

**Injury Prevention Cues:**
- "If the bind is not accessible, simply hold the opposite elbows behind the back while in Padmasana." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>
- "Rule: Never pull on the feet to force the bind; allow the hips to open naturally over time." <div class="citation-block">(raw PDF) </div>

## Teaching Notes
