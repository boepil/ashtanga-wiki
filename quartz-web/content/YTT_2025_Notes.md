# Yoga Teacher Training Notes (YTT 2025)

A Yoga Teacher Training (YTT) intensive is designed to be a "psychic hygiene" process. The journey requires a profound transition from being a performer of postures to an observer of the self, and eventually, a clear mirror for others.

## Learning Models

### The Gurukula Model
The traditional system where knowledge passes directly from teacher to student through body, mind, and heart. In this setting, the teacher acts as a "spiritual machinist," helping detect and correct misalignments in the student’s physical and philosophical understanding.

### Maintaining the Shishya (Student) Mindset
Professional stagnation is avoided when the teacher remains a "constant student."
- **Passion for Truth:** Be like an infant learning to walk—falling and getting up until the knowledge is integrated.
- **Humility:** Possess *Sraddha* (dynamic faith) and the patience required to calm old patterns (*Samskaras*).

## YTT Intensive Notes Template

**Training Week:** ________
**Focus Text / Theme:** ____________________

### 1. Daily Schedule (The Rhythm of Study)
Record your engagement with the structured intensive model (e.g., the "6-6-6-6" schedule):
- **04:00 – 05:00:** Personal Practice / Chanting / Meditation
- **05:00 – 08:00:** Asana Practice (Mysore Style or Led)
- **10:00 – 12:30:** Philosophy and Textual Analysis
- **14:30 – 17:00:** Asana Lab / Anatomy / Teaching Practicum
- **17:00 – 18:30:** Evening Meditation / Pranayama / Satsang

### 2. Philosophy & Textual Analysis
- **Key Sutra/Verse:** Record the transliteration, meaning, and how it applies to teaching.
- **Mental Patterns:** Identify the presence of the *Vrittis* or *Kleshas* discussed in lectures.

### 3. Asana Lab (Technical Breakdown)
- **Asana Name:** ____________________
- **Foundation & Risks:** Note what joints are most at risk (e.g., knees, lower back) and where stability originates.
- **Vinyāsa Krama:** Breakdown the precise breath count for entry, holding, and exit.
- **Hands-on Cues:** Record the "handles of the body" and the intended direction of energy for adjustments.

### 4. Teaching Practicum
- **Class Observation:** Notice the lead teacher's voice, pacing, and how they "see" the students.
- **Practice Teaching:** Log your experience guiding a peer. Did you use clear, direct action verbs?
- **Modifications:** Note specific modifications offered for different body types, proportions, or injuries.

### 5. Weekly Integration
- **The Guna Profile:** Reflect on your overall energy for the week—were you Sattvic, Rajasic, or exhausted (Tamasic)?
- **Aha! Moments:** Document breakthroughs in your understanding of pedagogy or personal practice.
